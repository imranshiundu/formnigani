How it works

Three taps from
nothing to something.

No group-chat chaos, no dead plans. One unit does all the work — the Form.

01

Drop a Form

Say what you're doing, when, and roughly where. Thirty seconds, one photo, done. It goes live to your squad and people nearby.

02

The squad piles in

Friends and nearby people hit Hype, Maybe, or I'm in. Comments keep the plan moving — what to bring, who's late, where exactly.

03

Show up

The exact spot unlocks when you're in, the WhatsApp group links up, and the weekend actually happens. Streak stays alive.
